How choices are rated

BestModels the Valor tool or value fully. Net +2 or more.
GoodHelpful, but not the strongest option. Net +1.
NeutralNo real effect either way.
PoorBlame, toxicity, guessing or poor balance. Net negative.

Listen, Mirror, Check

Timed, 12s · up to +3 Listening

Theo will make three calls. Each one shows for a few seconds, then it is gone. Listen fully, then pick the mirror and check you would say back.

Call 1
Theo: "Rotate right on the next timer, then hold the long corridor."Distraction: Jules: did you see that clip I sent??
  • So we rotate right on the next timer and I hold the long corridor? Is that right?
  • So we rotate left and hold the long corridor? Is that right?Wrong side. A mirror catches this before anyone moves.
  • Rotate right, then push the long corridor!"Hold" became "push", and there was no check.
  • Yep, got it.No mirror, so nobody knows what you heard.
Call 2
Theo: "Two on B, one on A. Save your shield for the retake."Distraction: Your phone buzzes on the desk.
  • So two on B, one on A, and I save my shield for the retake? Is that right?
  • So two on A, one on B, and shield now? Is that right?The numbers flipped. Distractions make the gaps bigger.
  • So they're all on B? Is that right?You filled in the gap with a guess. Good that you checked, though.
  • Okay, using my shield now!You acted on half the call.
Call 3
Theo: "Don't chase. Fall back to spawn and wait for Mako."Distraction: Jules: I can chase, they're weak!
  • So no chasing, fall back to spawn and wait for Mako? Is that right?
  • So chase with Jules, then wait for Mako?Jules was louder, so his idea got in. That is the assumption barrier.
  • So fall back and wait for Jules? Is that right?Right action, wrong person. The check would let Theo fix it.
  • Got it.No mirror, so nobody can tell if you heard it right.

CALM reset

Timed, 28s · up to +3 Influence

Tactical pause, 30 seconds. Jules is yelling, Theo has gone silent, Mako cannot talk, and you can hear your own voice getting louder.

C CheckNotice your own stress first
  • (Take one slow breath. Notice that your voice is loud too.)You cannot calm a team you are adding stress to.
  • (Go straight to telling everyone what they did wrong.)Skipping the check means your stress leads the call.
  • (Ignore how you feel and talk faster.)Faster is not calmer.
A AdjustChange your tone
  • Okay. We're fine. We're only two rounds behind.Slower and lower. People match the tone they hear.
  • EVERYONE CALM DOWN!Shouting "calm down" is never calm.
  • Ugh. Guys. Seriously.Frustration spreads just as fast as calm does.
L LimitOne main point
  • One job this round: hold B together. That's it.One point. Everyone can hold it in their head.

  • Theo, what are you seeing?Asking brings in information you did not have.
  • Nobody talk except me.Control, not influence. You lose what your team knows.
  • Any questions? No? Good.That is not space. That is a closed door.

Before the match

Jules is supportive unless the player's relationship with Jules is negative. Theo offers to help if Theo is 3 or more.

After the CALM reset

If CALM score is 50% or more AND (Theo is 2 or more OR the player checked Theo's call in Chapter 2), Theo shares that the other team leaves A empty. Mirroring it unlocks the winning call.

Winning the final

Average of 3P brief and CALM scores, plus a big bonus for the A call. 85% or more wins the grand final 13 to 11. Otherwise the Comets lose by two rounds, calmly.

The offer

Main roster shot caller if Coach is 2 or more and the four comms skills total 10 or more. Otherwise another academy season as deputy caller.
