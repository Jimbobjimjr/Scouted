How choices are rated

BestModels the Valor tool or value fully. Net +2 or more.
GoodHelpful, but not the strongest option. Net +1.
NeutralNo real effect either way.
PoorBlame, toxicity, guessing or poor balance. Net negative.

Green chips raise a score, red chips lower it. A purple "will remember that" tag shows a pop-up that the character remembers. "If the timer runs out" is what happens when the player says nothing. In the mini games, green is the target answer, amber is partly right and red misses the tool.

Hidden consequences

Energy

Every side activity costs energy (1 in the morning, 2 after school, 3 for late ranked). Sleep gives 3 back each night, up to 6. Fresh (5 or more) slows every timer by 20%. Running on empty (1 or less) speeds them up by 25% and takes 10% off call builder scores. Options without a cost earn nothing.

Relationships

They start low (Coach 2, Mako 2, Jules 1, Theo 1, Dee 3). At 15 someone is Trusted and gives a perk. Below 0 they are Strained and it costs the player. Dee drops 1 at the end of any day (from Day 2) where the player did not gain points with her.

Perks and strains

Coach: tip on the best first option, or 1 less skill point from minigames. Mako: team call builders 10% higher or lower. Jules: 6 seconds more or less on timed builders. Theo: mirror calls stay up longer or vanish sooner. Dee: 1 extra energy each morning, or plans with Dee are locked and she skips the final.

Carried forward

Writing settings down gives +1 Coach on Day 2. Grinding ranked sets the tired flag. Starting the poster with Kai pays off on Day 3.
