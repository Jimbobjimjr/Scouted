How choices are rated

BestModels the Valor tool or value fully. Net +2 or more.
GoodHelpful, but not the strongest option. Net +1.
NeutralNo real effect either way.
PoorBlame, toxicity, guessing or poor balance. Net negative.

Green chips raise a score, red chips lower it. Energy is shown but not counted in the rating. A purple "will remember that" tag shows a pop-up that the character remembers. "If the timer runs out" is what happens when the player says nothing. In the mini games, green is the target answer, amber is partly right and red misses the tool. In the game itself the options are shuffled every time.

Hidden consequences

Energy

Every side activity costs energy (1 in the morning, 2 after school, 3 for late ranked). Resting gives 1 back, and sleep gives 3 more each night, up to 6. Fresh (5 or more) slows every timer by 20%. Running on empty (1 or less) speeds them up by 25% and takes 10% off call builder scores.

Relationships

They start low (Coach 2, Mako 2, Jules 1, Theo 1, Dee 3). At 8 someone is Close and gives a small perk. At 15 they are Trusted and the perk is stronger. Below 0 they are Strained and it costs the player. Dee drops 1 at the end of any day (from Day 2) where the player did not gain points with her.

Perks and strains

Coach: Close rules out one weak option in the first step of each call builder, Trusted flags a strong one, Strained takes 1 skill point off minigames. Mako: team call builders score 5% (Close) or 10% (Trusted) higher, or 10% lower. Jules: 3 or 6 seconds more on timed builders, or 6 fewer. Theo: mirror calls stay up 0.75 or 1.5 seconds longer, or vanish 1 second sooner. Dee: Close gives 4 extra seconds on the first timed choice each day, Trusted gives 1 extra energy each morning, Strained locks plans with Dee and she skips the final.

Order and timers

The options in every choice are shuffled each time, so the order here is not the order on screen. Timers are set from how much there is to read (about 2 seconds plus a third of a second per word, 6 seconds at least) and stop while the Menu, Team or Log panel is open. The times shown here are for normal energy.

Carried forward

Writing settings down gives +1 Coach on Day 2. Grinding ranked sets the tired flag. Starting the poster with Kai softens the poster check on Day 5.

Recap lines: "How you used it today"
